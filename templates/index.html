🤔 What does this all mean?

Market Mood

A score from -1 (very scary/negative news) to +1 (very exciting/positive news). Think of it like the overall vibe of today's financial news.

Safe-Haven Pressure

How many articles mention "safe" assets like gold, bonds, or cash. High pressure = investors are getting nervous and moving money away from stocks.

Risk-On Activity

How many articles are talking about growth, rallies, and optimism. High = people are excited to invest. Low = caution in the air.

Tipping Point Alert

Fires when both the mood is negative AND lots of articles mention gold/bonds. This is the signal that the crowd may be heading for the exit.
